How SafeWatch
keeps you safe

Getting started takes less than 5 minutes. Follow these four simple steps to connect your neighborhood, report incidents, and stay informed with real-time community alerts.

Step 1

Sign up & join your neighborhood

Create your free account and enter your address. SafeWatch automatically connects you to your verified neighborhood group, HOA, or block watch team — no invitations needed.

Address Verification

Secure address confirmation ensures only real neighbors join your group.

Identity Protection

Your personal details are never shared with other residents without your consent.

Create Free Account
Step 2

Report incidents with one tap

Spot something suspicious? Report it instantly. Add a photo, pin the exact location on the map, and choose your urgency level. Your report goes live to neighbors in seconds.

Photo Evidence

Attach photos or videos directly to your incident report for greater context.

Anonymous Reports

Submit reports anonymously if you prefer privacy while keeping your community informed.

See Reporting Demo
Step 3

Receive real-time community alerts

Stay informed about everything happening in your neighborhood. SafeWatch delivers instant push notifications, SMS alerts, and email digests — exactly the way you want them.

  • Instant push notifications for urgent incidents
  • Daily or weekly email safety digest
  • Custom alert radius from 0.1 to 5 miles
View Alert Dashboard
Step 4

Connect & coordinate your community

Build a stronger neighborhood watch team. Create patrol groups, schedule volunteers, assign block captains, and communicate securely — all within SafeWatch.

Patrol Scheduling

Organize watch patrol routes and schedule volunteer time slots easily.

Group Messaging

Encrypted group chat for block captains, HOA boards, and watch teams.
