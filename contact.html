Frequently asked questions

How quickly will someone get back to me?
Our community team responds to all messages within one business day, usually the same afternoon for messages sent before 3PM EST. For urgent HOA onboarding or technical issues, call our support line — it's staffed Monday through Friday 8AM–8PM EST.
What do I need to set up SafeWatch for my HOA?
Getting started is simple. All we need is your HOA or neighborhood name, your general geographic boundaries (we use a boundary map tool — no technical skills needed), and one administrator email to set up the account. Most HOAs are fully live within 24 hours of signing up.
Can SafeWatch integrate with our existing HOA management software?
SafeWatch offers API integration with popular HOA management platforms. We also support single sign-on (SSO) for organizations that manage resident accounts centrally. Contact our partnerships team to discuss your specific setup — we can usually accommodate custom integration requests.
