Frequently asked questions

Is SafeWatch really free for residents?
Yes, completely. Individual residents can join SafeWatch, report incidents, receive community alerts, read safety tips, and join neighborhood groups — all at no cost, forever. We only charge HOAs and community organizations for advanced management and analytics tools.
How does SafeWatch protect my privacy?
Your personal information is never sold to third parties or shared with advertisers. All reports can be submitted anonymously. We use end-to-end encryption for all messages and reports. Your home address is verified but never displayed to other users — only your neighborhood zone is shared.
Can I cancel my HOA plan at any time?
Yes. All SafeWatch paid plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Your neighborhood group and historical data remain accessible even after cancellation.
How many residents can join a neighborhood group?
There is no limit on the number of residents who can join a neighborhood group on any plan. We believe the more connected your community, the safer it is. HOA Pro plans support multiple sub-groups, block divisions, and zone management for larger communities.
Does SafeWatch work with local police departments?
SafeWatch integrates with participating local law enforcement agencies through our SafeCity Partner Program. When enabled, verified incident reports can be automatically forwarded to the local non-emergency police line, with your permission. Contact us to learn if your department is a partner.
